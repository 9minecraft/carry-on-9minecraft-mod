# Carry On 9minecraft Mod - Move Full Chests Without Emptying

Carry On 9minecraft Mod lets you pick up chests, furnaces, machines, and even animals and villagers with your bare hands and carry them wherever you want - no tools, no breaking, and nothing spills out. Walk up to a block entity or a passive mob, hold the pickup key, and your character lifts it; place it back down exactly where you need it with another click. It is the perfect mod for reorganizing a base or moving a whole storage room without emptying every container by hand.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Purplicious_Cow, Tschipp**'s original work, kept running on current releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.
